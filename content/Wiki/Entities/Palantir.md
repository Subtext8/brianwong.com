# Palantir

Enterprise data/AI platform company known for its ontology-centred approach to operations.

## From [[Wiki/Sources/Is Ontology the AI Breakthrough That Actually Threatens Jobs|Is Ontology the AI Breakthrough That Actually Threatens Jobs]]
- Alongside Databricks, one of the influential AI/data companies highlighting ontology as important for effective AI systems.
- Treats ontology as more than a data model: connects real-world objects and relationships with functions, actions and security.
- "It isn't merely describing the business. It is creating a machine-readable version of how the business operates."
