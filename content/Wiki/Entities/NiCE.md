# NiCE

Customer experience / contact-centre software company with a forward-deployed engineering team building AI agents for clients.

## From [[Wiki/Sources/Why AI Agents Orchestrators Are In High Demand|Why AI Agents Orchestrators Are In High Demand]]
- Forward-deployed engineers are embedded directly with the customer — their engineers and staff — to learn how the business works, then build and orchestrate agents alongside people.
- Woody Holdmere leads the forward-deployed engineering team.
- Example project: car insurance quoting takes 30+ steps to gather enough information; the team workshops what data is needed, gathers it via Q&A or document upload, and builds an agent to cut the time.
- See [[Wiki/Concepts/AI Orchestration|AI Orchestration]].
