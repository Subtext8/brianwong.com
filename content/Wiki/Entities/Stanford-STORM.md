# Stanford-STORM (STORM)

STORM (Synthesis of Topic Outlines through Retrieval and Multi-perspective Question Asking) is an LLM system that automates the pre-writing stage of generating grounded, long-form, Wikipedia-like articles.

## From [[Wiki/Sources/Stanford-STORM|Stanford-STORM]]
- Automates research by simulating conversations between a Wikipedia writer and a topic expert.
- Uses perspective-guided question asking to improve depth and breadth.
- Implemented using [[Wiki/Entities/DSPy|DSPy]].

## From [[Wiki/Sources/STORM-paper|STORM-paper]]
- Models the pre-writing stage: discover diverse perspectives, simulate conversations with topic experts, curate information into an outline.
- Significantly outperforms baseline [[Wiki/Concepts/RAG|RAG]] methods in article organization and coverage.
