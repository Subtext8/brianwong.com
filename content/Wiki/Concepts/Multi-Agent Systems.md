# Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)

Teams of specialized agents collaborating.

## From [[Wiki/Sources/The Artificial Intelligence Industry 2026|The Artificial Intelligence Industry 2026]]
- Vs super-agent; research/code/QA teams; cross-vendor protocols; 15% autonomous decisions (2028).

## From [[Wiki/Sources/Why AI Agents Orchestrators Are In High Demand|Why AI Agents Orchestrators Are In High Demand]]
- Orchestration sits above MAS: someone must decide which agent handles what, constrain behavior, and measure value.
- Handoff pattern: a primary assistant agent detects when a specialized agent is needed, captures context, hands off, and resumes after resolution.
- "For every agent, you might need a workforce that actually manages those agents" — agent teams create orchestration roles, not just fewer people.
- See [[Wiki/Concepts/AI Orchestration|AI Orchestration]].
