# AI Orchestration

The work of deciding how tasks are split between people and [[Wiki/Concepts/AI Agents|AI agents]] so an entire business process gets done, then building, connecting, monitoring and measuring the agent team that runs it.

## From [[Wiki/Sources/Why AI Agents Orchestrators Are In High Demand|Why AI Agents Orchestrators Are In High Demand]]
- **Job, skill and capability**: "it's really all three." Everyone is expected to manage multiple agents; a dedicated orchestrator role is senior.
- **Air traffic controller analogy**: the controller doesn't fly the plane — they make sure many simultaneous flights don't collide. Orchestrators constrain and coordinate rather than personally do each task.
- **What the role covers**: designing and managing agents, async coordination across an ecosystem, optimizing, maintaining, reviewing, analytics and reporting value to the business.
- **Handoffs**: a primary agent recognizes when a specialized agent is needed, captures context, hands off, then resumes when the sub-outcome is resolved.
- **Human strengths that stay central**: building systems, understanding risk, building trust, testing and guardrails — "AI is not always predictable."
- **Human-to-human-to-agent workforce**: the operating model shifts from people-to-people to people-to-people-to-agents.
- **Tooling**: [[Wiki/Concepts/MCP|MCP]], LangGraph, Semantic Kernel; the terms agent-to-agent and agent-to-UI describe the connection patterns.
- **Skills mix**: software engineering, systems integration, customer-facing work, process thinking, commercial judgment — plus creative, curious problem solving. Soft skills are the career differentiator.
- **Market**: US agentic AI roles average $115k–$239k; postings grew ~15× in four years ([[Wiki/Entities/Randstad|Randstad]]) and +1,700% in banking ([[Wiki/Entities/Draup|Draup]]) while supply grew only ~300%.
- **Career outlook**: one orchestrator becomes a team, then a business unit — growth rather than simple substitution, though employees fear replacement (Glassdoor negativity 15% → 53%, 2019–2026).
