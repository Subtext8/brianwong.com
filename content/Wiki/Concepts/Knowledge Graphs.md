# Knowledge Graphs

Labeled directed graphs used since early AI for knowledge representation.

## From [[Wiki/Sources/How Knowledge Graphs Relate to AI|How Knowledge Graphs Relate to AI]]
- Symbiotic with AI: Enable apps (personal assistants, recommender systems, search engines); built using AI (entity/relation extraction, schema mapping, entity linking, data cleaning, inference, NLQA).
- Three intersections: Test-bed for current AI algos, Graph Data Science enabler, core for long-term AI vision (explicit, expressive reps).

## From [[Wiki/Sources/Is Ontology the AI Breakthrough That Actually Threatens Jobs|Is Ontology the AI Breakthrough That Actually Threatens Jobs]]
- KGs represent things and their relationships instead of isolated text chunks: a customer placed an order → order contains a product → product stored at a warehouse → warehouse supplied by a manufacturer.
- Used alongside retrieval in [[Wiki/Concepts/GraphRAG|GraphRAG]] ([[Wiki/Entities/Microsoft|Microsoft]]).
- Limit: a connection doesn't say what two things *mean* in an organisation or what the AI may do with them — the gap [[Wiki/Concepts/Ontology|ontology]] fills.
